## 1. Summary

The dissertation builds a spell checker for Maltese that combines a finite-state lexicon with a neural re-ranker trained on a newly annotated corpus of learner and web text (§3.1, p. 21). It reports a correction accuracy of 87.4% on a held-out test set and compares against Hunspell and a character-level baseline (§5.2, p. 48).
